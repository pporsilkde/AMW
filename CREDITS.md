# Credits

ArenaMW is built on the work of the OpenMW community and on compatible systems developed in the TES3MP/ArenaMP/EncoreMP ecosystem.

- OpenMW provides the open-source Morrowind engine foundation; historical contributors are retained in `AUTHORS.md`.
- TES3MP/ArenaMP work is the source of a number of systems later converted to local standalone ownership in ArenaMW.
- EncoreMP optional content is retained under `extras/EncoreMP` with its companion documentation.
- Intel's MaskedOcclusionCulling source is retained under `extern/maskedoc` with its source notices.

No upstream project or Bethesda Softworks endorses ArenaMW.
